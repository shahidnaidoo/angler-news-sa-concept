## Source and reuse boundaries

- All seven new downloads succeeded from the exact image URLs linked by their reporting pages. Normal browser user-agent and ECR referer headers were needed for the image host. No guessed source-photo variants were substituted.
- Page facts, captions and image subjects corroborate these selections. Measurements are **reported by the source**, not independently measured here. A large catch is not automatically a certified record. Avoid carrying a source's time-sensitive weekend/weather claims into the current site as live advice.
- Displayed copy suggestions are brief original paraphrases, not article reproductions. Each recommended story has a separate primary page. Retain source links and keep any expanded extract within the applicable source word limit; do not paste full articles into cards/details.
- Downloading is not a reuse license. ANSA/ECR branding and photographs remain with their respective owners. No photographer, subject or business release was obtained. Preserve source credits and the independent-pitch label. Any official/commercial business release requires appropriate rights authorization; no MIT grant applies to third-party assets.
- Only `docs/EXPANSION-SOURCES.md` and the specifically authorized new `work/expansion-assets/` downloads were created by this research pass. Existing website implementation files were not edited.
