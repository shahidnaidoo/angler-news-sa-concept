# ANSA independent pitch: design brief

## Purpose

Make ANSA's real local knowledge easier for a fisherman to use: find the relevant region, explore the kind of fishing they enjoy, and listen to the authentic report. This public independent pitch is a reviewable mock-up, not an official ANSA service. Preserve accurate branding and source links, and clearly distinguish sample interactions from current conditions.

## Editorial direction

Use a **catalog/detail** structure: a confident editorial homepage with searchable, filterable content cards that open concise detail views. Bring dated reports, fishing facets, locations and the real podcast into one coherent journey. Prioritize a small number of useful interactions over decorative dashboard widgets.

## Visual language

- **Palette:** ink navy foundations, authentic ANSA red accents, warm white editorial surfaces, restrained gray metadata.
- **Typography:** oversized condensed headings with a clear supporting sans serif; bold, short, plain-language titles and highly readable report copy.
- **Photography:** photo-led hero using authentic source material; preserve fish, hands and faces in crops. Use verified ANSA archive photographs and credit their provenance. No generated concept imagery per the CLIO opt-out.
- **Layout:** generous editorial spacing, strong rules, simple rectangular cards, decisive scale contrast, and a responsive mobile flow.
- **Brand:** retain the actual ANSA logo and “Let's Fish for the Future” conservation connection. Do not invent endorsements, audience statistics, testimonials or partnerships.

## Homepage and core interactions

1. **Photo-led hero:** an immediate fishing-focused message, a clear explore-reports action and a real listen action. Show the independent concept status discreetly but visibly.
2. **Dated reports catalog:** source-backed editorial excerpts or explicitly labeled example content; visible dates, region and fishing facet; local search/filter controls with clear reset and empty states.
3. **Region explorer:** Durban, North Coast, South Coast and Midlands pathways. Selecting a region updates useful local content and provides an obvious route to detail.
4. **Report detail:** accessible panel/dialog with descriptive heading, image credit, readable summary and official source/listen link. Support keyboard dismissal and clear focus handling.
5. **Official audio:** click-to-load the existing iono.fm channel `https://iframe.iono.fm/c/8652`; link to the ECR hub and podcast platforms as reliable fallbacks. Never simulate audio playback.
6. **Archive gallery:** authentic catches labeled as ANSA editorial archive, with captions and source credits. Do not imply these images were uploaded through the mock-up.
7. **Community and follow:** verified WhatsApp, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok links. Use honest destination labels; avoid fake signup or submission success.

## Verification and publication

Verify desktop and mobile layout, menu behavior, filter/search changes and reset, detail opening/closing, official audio loading, gallery interactions and external destinations. Check keyboard access and obvious visual contrast. Publish the tested code to the user's GitHub account and Vercel only with actual returned deployment evidence; verify the live page and share the real URL. Keep public concept/rights status and research provenance inspectable in the repository.
